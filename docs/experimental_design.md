# Experimental Design

Every experiment should specify:

- Research question and hypothesis.
- Primary and secondary outcomes.
- Baseline implementations and their settings.
- Network topology and workload generation.
- Failure and supply-shock distributions.
- Number of independent replications and random seeds.
- Compute budget and runtime measurement procedure.
- Statistical analysis and uncertainty reporting.
- Exclusion rules and known limitations.

Do not report target KPIs as results before executing the corresponding experiments.
