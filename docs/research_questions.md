# Research Questions

RQ1. Under what assumptions can local edge reinforcement, decay, and reconnection improve
network throughput after link failures?

RQ2. How does the proposed allocation rule compare with well-specified optimization baselines
under the same constraints and computational budget?

RQ3. Can decentralized exchange maintain acceptable allocation efficiency and fairness under
localized supply shocks?

RQ4. What are the computational and communication costs of the proposed framework as network
size, failure rate, and resource volatility increase?

These questions are provisional and should be refined after the literature review.
