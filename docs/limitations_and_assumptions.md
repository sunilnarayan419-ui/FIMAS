# Limitations and Assumptions

- Biological mechanisms may be simplified or mapped imperfectly to engineered systems.
- Synthetic graphs may not represent real operational networks.
- Simulation findings do not by themselves establish field performance.
- Results may depend on hyperparameters and baseline implementation quality.
- Fairness and efficiency may conflict.
- Decentralization does not automatically eliminate communication overhead or failure modes.
- All claims require traceable evidence and reproducible tests.
