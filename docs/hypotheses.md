# Hypotheses

All hypotheses below are prospective and must be tested.

- H1: Dynamic reinforcement and decay may improve post-failure throughput relative to a
  specified static-topology baseline under defined graph and failure distributions.
- H2: The proposed optimizer may improve a predefined objective under a matched evaluation
  budget; convergence and solution quality must both be reported.
- H3: Decentralized exchange may preserve specified fairness and feasibility bounds under
  supply shocks, relative to an explicit market baseline.
- H4: Any performance gains may depend on network density, failure distribution, parameter
  settings, and communication overhead.

For each hypothesis, specify primary outcome, comparator, sample size or replication plan,
random seeds, uncertainty interval, and falsification criterion before running experiments.
