# Objectives and Scope

## In scope

- Dynamic graph and edge-state models.
- Resource-flow and allocation constraints.
- Candidate fungal-inspired optimization rules.
- Agent-based resource exchange and fairness.
- Reproducible simulation and baseline evaluation.

## Out of scope for the initial scaffold

- Physical micro-grid control.
- Claims of production readiness.
- Unverified performance guarantees.
- Mandatory deep learning or distributed deployment before a baseline simulator exists.
